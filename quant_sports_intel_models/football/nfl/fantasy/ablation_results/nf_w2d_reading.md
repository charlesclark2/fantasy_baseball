## ⭐ The finding that outlives the story: the as-of-capture era prices a SMALLER lift at RB

The pre-registered era-delta diagnostic, read with an exact rank statistic (post-hoc, never a
gate; BH q=0.10 applied across the four positions because four p-values are reported):

| RB | +0.1347 | **+0.0422** | 5.16% → **1.72%** | **24/24** | **0.0110** | 0.025 | **yes** |
| WR | +0.1257 | +0.0840 | 4.49% → 3.26% | 20/24 | 0.0989 | 0.050 | no |
| TE | +0.0589 | +0.0420 | 3.17% → 2.26% | 19/24 | 0.1319 | 0.075 | no |

**At RB both 2025 folds fall below EVERY one of the twelve legacy folds** (legacy range
[+0.0760, +0.1913]; 2025 = +0.0421, +0.0424). WR is suggestive and does not survive BH; TE and QB
show nothing — QB's 2025 lift is if anything larger, and its legacy fold-to-fold SD (0.058) is
wide enough that neither 2025 fold is remarkable.

Three candidate explanations were **eliminated with measurements already in hand**:

1. **Coverage dilution — refuted.** 2025H1 is **100% covered** and posts RB +0.0421; 2025H2 is
   89.77% covered and posts +0.0424. The fully-covered fold shows the attenuation *identically*,
   so the week-12 hole is not the cause.
2. **Season difficulty / scale — refuted for RB.** 2025 was a lower-CRPS season for RB and WR
   (RB `base_rate` 2.613 → 2.450), so a smaller absolute lift is partly arithmetic. Normalising
   by the foil's own CRPS, RB still falls **5.16% → 1.72%**, a ~3× attenuation that scale does
   not explain.
3. **Practice-line absence — refuted as the explanation for the POSITION pattern.** The tempting
   story: 34% of listed 2025 rows have no practice line, and the two attenuating positions
   (RB, WR) are exactly the two whose winner is `inj_both` — the arm that feeds the family into
   BOTH legs — while the two clean positions (QB, TE) run `inj_zero_leg`. That pattern is real
   and it is a coincidence. If arm sensitivity drove it, `inj_both`'s era ratio would sit below
   `inj_zero_leg`'s; measured, the era ratio is **arm-INVARIANT** (RB 0.313 / 0.373 / 0.312 across
   `inj_both` / `inj_override` / `inj_zero_leg`; max |`inj_both` − `inj_zero_leg`| across all four
   positions = **0.062**). The attenuation is a property of the POSITION, not of how much of the
   family an arm consumes. *(NF-D15 (g′), one step earlier: a pattern lining up with an arm is
   not that arm causing it — and this one was checked rather than shipped as a mechanism.)*

**What survives, unrefuted and not separable by this run:** (i) the as-of-capture SIGNAL
CHARACTER itself — an archive capture is an observation at that instant, not the final Friday
designation (NF-W2c measured ESPN agreement decaying 0.80 at day-1 to ~0.3–0.5 at day-4+, the
dominant disagreement being the canonical `questionable → out` mid-week downgrade). This would
attenuate the availability signal *uniformly across arms*, which is what is observed; (ii) a
genuine RB-specific 2025 difference; (iii) n = 2 folds. Note that (i) predicts uniformity but not
by itself the *per-position* spread, so it is the leading candidate rather than the answer.

**Why this matters for serving, stated plainly:** the family SHIPS on the serve-adjacent era, and
that is the de-risking result the story was run for. But the honest magnitude estimate for a live
2026 feed built from as-of captures is the 2025 figure, not the legacy one — at RB that is
**+0.042 CRPS/wk, roughly a third of the +0.135 the vendor-stamped era priced.** A 2026 forecast
of the injury lever's size should quote the 2025 column of the table above, per position.

## Guards (66, all RED-proven)

`betting_ml/tests/test_nf_w2d_2025_regate.py`. Every RED-proof mutates the module source
IN-PROCESS and **asserts the mutation landed** before running the guard (E11.24 #682 — a
RED-proof that can silently no-op reports a false "the guard caught it"): dropping the
declared-absence reason must make the PIT guard reject; laundering the capture instant into the
vendor slot must be detectable; relaxing the strict-before bound must admit a gameday-instant
stamp; `fillna(0)` on an unobserved row must produce the healthy zero the live code refuses; a
wrong practice key map must zero the family; widening the coverage bound must "cover" the
Thanksgiving week the data cannot support.

Three defects the guards found and fixed while building:

1. **A structurally unreachable guard.** `combine_injury_sources` filtered BOTH sources into
   disjointness and *then* checked for overlap — a check neither side could ever trip. Fixed by
   making the filters deliberately asymmetric: stampless post-2024 nflverse rows are EXCLUDED
   (they genuinely exist and are genuinely inadmissible), a pre-2025 capture row is REFUSED. The
   overlap check is now reachable and both branches are tested.
2. **A guard matching prose instead of a call site.** "NF-W2d must not call `deflated_sharpe`
   itself" matched the runner's own explanatory string. Fixed to a call-site regex
   (`M14\.deflated_sharpe\s*\(`) with a vacuity assertion that the regex matches where a call DOES
   exist (DSR-CONV #690).
3. **A RED-proof that could not isolate its clause.** The capture branch's own strict `< g`
   pre-filter cannot be RED-proven alone, because `admissible` refuses the same row a line later.
   Rather than present a guard that cannot fail, the suite RED-proves the load-bearing bound on
   the LEGACY path (which bypasses the pre-filter entirely, so the strict bound is the only clause
   that can flip) and adds an explicit test stating that the pre-filter is redundant
   defence-in-depth — proving the pair is load-bearing by relaxing BOTH clauses together
   (NF-D17: a fixture that trips a second clause proves nothing about the first).
