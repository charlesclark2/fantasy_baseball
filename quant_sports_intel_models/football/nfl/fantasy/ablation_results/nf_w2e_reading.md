# NF-W2e — reading the capture-freshness study

Narrative companion to the generated artifact `nf_w2e_capture_freshness.{md,json}` (the
authoritative record; every number below is generated by the runner, not hand-transcribed).
Pre-registration: `nf_w2e_preregistration.md`, committed before scoring (`8221fb26`).
`best_alpha = 0`; **deploy-held — this story promotes nothing, publishes nothing, retrains nothing,
and does not change NF-W2d's feature construction.**

**The question.** NF-W2d found that the as-of-capture era prices a ~3× smaller lift at RB
(+0.0422 in 2025 vs +0.1347 across the twelve legacy folds; both 2025 folds below every legacy
fold; exact p = 0.0110, surviving BH). NF-W2d could not separate *"an archive capture is a stale
photograph of the injury table"* from *"2025 was genuinely different at RB."* NF-W2e attacks the
first half with data already landed: the store carries a capture instant per row, so the
consumption rule can be tightened and the lift re-read against capture age.

## Verdict, and why it was fixed before the run

**`NO_CERTIFICATION_POSSIBLE` at all four positions — registered in advance, not chosen after.**
The consumption rule can only act on capture-era rows, so the active-fold count is a *design*
quantity: **2**. At 2 folds `cv_power` reports the fold-consistency clause unattainable, PBO
**undefined** (not failed), the sign floor 0.25 (above `FDR_Q`), and `dsr_ceiling` **0.9214**
against a 0.95 gate — i.e. **no effect of any size could clear these gates.** Registering a
certification verdict would have been theatre, so the story registered a row-level *reading*
instead: a week-**clustered** paired delta over the 8,688 capture-era rows, with the fold-level
CRPS reported and never gated.

This is the NF-D20 discipline applied before the fact: count the folds on which the mechanism can
*act* before crediting or condemning anything.

## Controls (all PASS — the run is valid)

| control | result |
|---|---|
| The ladder is **inert before 2025** (mechanical) | PASS — 28 cells, 75,865 legacy rows, zero differences |
| The **scored population is identical** across rungs | PASS — 84,553 rows / 84,105 observed at every rung |
| Rows are **aligned** across rungs (paired deltas valid) | PASS |
| **Measured** tie on the registered legacy folds 2024H1/H2 | PASS — 16 comparisons, max \|Δ\| = **0.0** exactly |

The second is the load-bearing one. Coverage and consumption are deliberately *separate*: coverage
always uses NF-W2d's 7-day bound, and only *consumption* moves down the ladder. Had coverage
followed consumption, tightening the rung would have changed which rows are observed at all, and
every rung comparison would confound the feature with the population. A RED-proof collapses the two
and shows the population control firing.

## ⭐ The stratifier was validated first — and the first cut was wrong

MH2.1's rule: before reading anything off a per-stratum table, prove the bins separate something
real. Here the stratifier is capture age and the thing it must separate is *designation change* —
how often the designation seen at capture differs from the one seen later in the same week.

**The first cut said the stratifier was inert: rank corr −0.0026, p = 0.947.** Taken at face value
that is a clean `INACTIVE` finding — the mechanism has nothing to act on, close the story. It was
an artifact. nfl.com publishes practice-only rows with a NULL `report_status`, so a pair of
captures that crosses between a designation-publishing source and a practice-publishing one scores
as a "change" that is a **source-format difference, not a designation change**. 286 of 386 pairs
were that.

Restricted to pairs where both captures carry a real designation (n = 341):

| bin | n | change rate | SE |
|---|---|---|---|
| <0.5 d | 52 | 0.0385 | 0.0267 |
| 0.5–1 d | 66 | 0.2576 | 0.0538 |
| 1–2 d | 84 | 0.3333 | 0.0514 |
| 2–3 d | 10 | 0.2000 | 0.1265 |
| >3 d | 129 | 0.3953 | 0.0430 |

**VALIDATED** — rank corr **+0.289**, p < 1e-4; two-bin cut 0.161 (≤1 d) vs 0.363 (>1 d), which is
what fixes the ladder's middle rung at exactly 1 day. Both cuts are published in the artifact so
the reason the corrected one is trustworthy is visible rather than asserted.

*The transferable lesson (a new costume for the MH2.1 rule):* the stratifier-validation step can
itself be confounded, and its failure mode is a **false `INACTIVE`** — the most closable-looking
verdict there is. A multi-source panel makes this the default risk: the comparison crossed a
*source-format* boundary, not a *content* boundary, and nothing in the arithmetic says so.

## The ladder

Three rungs, identical in every respect except which admissible captures a row may consume.
Coverage is held at 7 days throughout.

| rung | consumption bound | listed capture-era rows | share of incumbent |
|---|---|---|---|
| `inj_latest` (incumbent) | ≤ 7 d | 1,343 | 1.000 |
| `inj_fresh1d` | ≤ 1 d | 475 | 0.354 |
| `inj_freshest` | the row's own freshest admissible instant | 856 | 0.637 |

`inj_freshest` is the **carry-over test**: it drops only the 36.3% of listed rows that are
*superseded* — a later admissible capture exists for that row and the incumbent used the older one
(median carry-over gap 1.65 d, p90 4.08 d). That configuration is structurally impossible in the
legacy era (one nflverse report per player-week), so it is a capture-regime property, not a defect.

## PRIMARY — week-clustered paired deltas (positive = the tighter rung beats the incumbent)

| rung | pooled Δ | clustered SE | CI95 | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `inj_fresh1d` | **−0.0352** | 0.0179 | [−0.0702, −0.0002] | **loses** (excludes zero) |
| `inj_freshest` | **−0.0008** | 0.0073 | [−0.0150, +0.0135] | **ties** (spans zero) |

Clustering matters: the SE inflation over the naive one is 2.12× pooled (and up to 1.72× per
position). On the naive SE the `inj_fresh1d` loss would read as far more decisive than it is.

Per position, `inj_fresh1d`: WR −0.0477 (excludes zero) · QB −0.0662 · RB −0.0179 · TE −0.0116
(all three span zero). `inj_freshest`: RB **+0.0136** (excludes zero) · QB +0.0021 · TE +0.0017 ·
WR −0.0130 (all span zero).

**Multiplicity, honestly:** BH at q = 0.10 over the 8 reported per-position comparisons (the pooled
`ALL` rows are excluded so they cannot inflate the correction) leaves **exactly one survivor —
RB/`inj_freshest`, +0.0136, p = 0.0099 against a 0.0125 cutoff.** WR/`inj_fresh1d` (p = 0.048)
does **not** survive. So the sharp per-position claim available here is "at RB, dropping superseded
designations helps slightly," and the "aggressive filtering hurts" statement is a *pooled-interval*
reading, not a BH-surviving per-position one. At 2 active folds none of this certifies anything in
either direction, which is the registered verdict.

## SECONDARY — the pre-registered stratified read

Lift over the matched foil, by the age of the designation the row actually consumed:

| position | listed, fresh ≤1 d | listed, stale >1 d | not listed |
|---|---|---|---|
| QB | **+1.832** ± 0.549 | +0.620 ± 0.399 | +0.004 ± 0.025 |
| RB | **+0.801** ± 0.354 | +0.113 ± 0.137 | −0.007 ± 0.009 |
| WR | **+0.884** ± 0.167 | +0.242 ± 0.099 | −0.005 ± 0.010 |
| TE | **+0.656** ± 0.127 | +0.005 ± 0.141 | +0.005 ± 0.006 |

The gradient is large and one-directional at every position: a fresh designation carries **3–7×**
the advantage of a stale one. The `not_listed` column is ≈ 0 everywhere — a built-in sanity check
that the read is measuring the designation channel and not a population artifact.

## ⭐ Reconciling the two halves — they are not in tension

A fresh designation is worth several times a stale one (stratified read), yet **dropping** stale
designations makes the model worse (`inj_fresh1d` loses) and dropping only superseded ones changes
nothing (`inj_freshest` ties). Both are true because the comparison is different:

> A stale designation is worth **less** than a fresh one — and **more** than none at all.

`inj_fresh1d` throws away 65% of the designations to buy freshness on the rest, and the rows it
blanks fall back to "not listed," which the third column shows is worth ≈ 0. `inj_freshest` throws
away only genuinely superseded ones and the model is indifferent, i.e. **carry-over is refuted as a
mechanism**: the incumbent's occasional use of an older-but-admissible capture is not what costs
NF-W2d its 2025 lift.

Reported as a two-way BEATS/LOSES word, that `inj_freshest` tie would have read as a *loss*, i.e.
as weak evidence that carrying stale designations **helps** — the opposite conclusion off identical
arithmetic. See the guards section.

## ⭐ The cross-era check — freshness cannot be NF-W2d's era explanation

A within-era gradient licenses an era *explanation* only if the eras differ on that variable in the
right direction. On the validated boundary they differ the **wrong** way:

| era | listed rows | ≤1 d | >1 d | median age | SD of age | >2 d |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| legacy (nflverse `date_modified`) | 14,058 | **0.0756** | 0.9244 | 1.435 d | 0.439 | 0.0394 |
| capture (2025) | 1,343 | **0.3537** | 0.6463 | 1.483 d | 1.375 | **0.2695** |

**2025 is the fresher era on this cut — 35.4% of its listed rows sit inside a day of gameday
against 7.6% for the vendor-stamped era.** Weighted by the stratified read's own per-stratum
lifts, that mix predicts a *larger* 2025 lift, not a smaller one. A simple freshness deficit
therefore cannot be why 2025's lift is smaller.

Three things this does **not** settle, stated because the same table shows them:

1. **The eras differ in dispersion, not location.** Median age is nearly identical (1.435 vs
   1.483 d) while the SD triples (0.439 → 1.375) — the capture era is *bimodal*, holding both more
   very-fresh rows and a much heavier stale tail (**27.0% >2 d vs 3.9%**). The registered two-bin
   cut prices "stale >1 d" as one stratum, so a further decay *inside* that stratum is not excluded
   by this comparison. The only bound available without an unregistered third bin is the
   stratifier-validation table's own proxy, where the change rate rises only modestly past two days
   (1–2 d 0.333 → >3 d 0.395), which argues the residual channel is small rather than absent.
   Computing a >2 d lift bin post-hoc is exactly the knob E2.1-r forbids, so it is left for a
   successor to pre-register.
2. **Stamp age is not the same quantity in the two eras.** A legacy `date_modified` is the report's
   own timestamp — the definitive designation, dated when it was issued. A capture instant is when
   the page was **photographed**, and the page may already have been displaying older content. The
   comparison therefore bounds the freshness explanation rather than settling it.
3. The within-era gradient's *strength* does not transfer across that boundary either, for the
   same reason.

⇒ **Freshness is a real and large within-2025 effect, and on the measurable cut it is not the era
explanation.** NF-W2d's RB attenuation survives this study unexplained — but the candidate list is
shorter by its most plausible member, and the survivors are now nameable: a genuine 2025 RB
difference; the as-of-capture signal *character* beyond mere age (an archive page that never showed
the final Friday designation at all — the `questionable → out` downgrade NF-W2c measured); the
heavier >2 d tail in (1); and n = 2 folds.

## Re-test trigger — calendar-bound, and say so

The binding constraint is the **active-fold count**, and it grows only with forward capture: the
capture era gains ~2 folds per season of NF-W0a live arming, so **PBO becomes evaluable at 4 folds
(end of 2026)** and the sign floor drops below `FDR_Q` there. This is not "needs more rows" — the
2025 rows are all present. It is a MH2 `POWER-LIMITED` shortfall stated in the unit that grows,
with the honest note that it is **not reachable now** by any re-analysis of landed data.

## Guards (44, RED-proven)

`betting_ml/tests/test_nf_w2e_capture_freshness.py`. Every RED-proof mutates the source IN-PROCESS
and **asserts the mutation landed** before running the guard (E11.24 #682): collapsing coverage
into consumption must shrink the observed population and be caught; a rung leaking into the legacy
era must fire the inertness control; dropping the cluster correction must collapse the SE onto the
naive one; a verdict constant claiming certification must be caught; moving the middle rung off the
validated boundary must be caught.

Two things the guards forced:

1. **An isolating fixture, twice.** The coverage/consumption RED-proof first used a week whose
   freshest capture was 0.2 d old — covered under *either* bound, so the mutation was inert and the
   "proof" proved nothing. Fixed by adding a week whose only capture is 3 days old: covered at 7 d,
   uncovered at 1 d. Same for the legacy-leak proof (NF-D17: a fixture that a second clause already
   rejects proves nothing about the first).
2. **A reporting defect caught late: a two-way direction word.** The verdict text said
   `BEATS if mean_delta > 0 else LOSES TO` — two-way, so a CI spanning zero could only come out as
   a **loss**, and it duly narrated the real `inj_freshest` tie (−0.0008, CI [−0.015, +0.013]) as
   "LOSES TO the incumbent." That is the NF1.8 flip-distribution error in prose: a direction
   statistic cannot separate "worse" from "indistinguishable," and here the distinction is the
   entire carry-over conclusion. Now a three-way `direction_word` that **fails closed to TIES** on
   an absent/unevaluable interval (NF1.7 (a)), with one isolating fixture per clause and a RED-proof
   feeding the real pooled read to the pre-fix wording to show it inverts the finding.

   Two things fell out of fixing it, and both are the more generalisable half:

   - **The word and the parenthetical have to fail closed together.** The first fix made the word
     say `TIES` while the parenthetical still read "excludes zero" — a self-contradicting sentence,
     and the half a reader trusts is the parenthetical. Caught by the guard, not by eye.
   - ⭐ **A verdict sentence must be DERIVED, not stored.** `verdict_text` was written once at
     scoring time, so correcting the wording would have cost a full refit to reach the artifact —
     which is exactly the pressure that leaves a known-wrong sentence in a published record.
     It is now a pure function of (validity, controls, clustered reads), re-derived by
     `--reanalyze` at zero refit cost, with a guard asserting **both** paths call it.

`--reanalyze` regenerates the reading aids (multiplicity, cross-era shares, verdict text) from the
stored per-fold scores plus a ~40 s matrix rebuild — no model fitting, no selection — and REFUSES
to write if a verdict, control, power ceiling or clustered read moved. It is how the artifact in
this directory was brought level with the final code without re-running the 17-minute study.
